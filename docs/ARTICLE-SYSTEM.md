# Negaresh Yar V1 - Article System Specification

## Purpose

This document defines the AI-assisted article creation workflow for Negaresh Yar V1.

The goal is to create useful, high-quality, SEO-friendly Persian content that helps users and improves organic search visibility.

---

# Article System Philosophy

The system is not designed for mass AI content generation.

The goal is:

- Helpful content.
- Natural writing.
- Real user value.
- Search engine compatibility.

---

# Article Workflow

The article creation process:
Topic Selection

↓

Search Intent Analysis

↓

Keyword Selection

↓

Article Structure Creation

↓

Content Generation

↓

AI Review

↓

Score Calculation

↓

Human Review

↓

Publishing

---

# Step 1 - Topic Selection

The administrator provides:

- Article topic
- Main service relation


The system should evaluate:

- User search intent.
- Business relevance.
- SEO opportunity.


---

# Step 2 - Keyword Planning

Every article must define:

- Main keyword.
- Secondary keywords.
- Search intent.


Rules:

- Keywords must be natural.
- No keyword stuffing.
- Content quality has priority.


---

# Step 3 - Article Structure

Before writing content, create:

- H1 title.
- H2 sections.
- H3 subsections.
- FAQ section.
- Conclusion.


---

# Step 4 - Content Generation

Articles must be:

- Written in Persian.
- Natural and readable.
- Useful for real users.
- Professionally structured.


Avoid:

- Robotic writing.
- Repetitive sentences.
- Generic AI explanations.


---

# AI Review System

Every article must be evaluated by multiple reviewers.


## Reviewer 1 - SEO Reviewer

Checks:

- Title optimization.
- Keyword usage.
- Heading structure.
- Meta information.
- Internal linking.


Output:

SEO Score (0-100)


---

## Reviewer 2 - Content Reviewer

Checks:

- Accuracy.
- Completeness.
- User value.
- Practical information.


Output:

Content Score (0-100)


---

## Reviewer 3 - Quality Reviewer

Checks:

- Natural language.
- Readability.
- AI writing patterns.
- Professional tone.


Output:

Quality Score (0-100)


---

# Final Score

Formula:
Final Score =
(SEO Score + Content Score + Quality Score) / 3


---

# Publishing Rule

An article can be published only when:


Final Score >= 80


---

# Article Status

Available statuses:


draft

review

approved

published

---

# Article SEO Requirements

Every published article must have:

- SEO title.
- Meta description.
- Clean slug.
- Featured image.
- Structured headings.
- Internal links.


---

# Google Search Console

After publishing:

The system should support:

- URL indexing workflow.
- Performance monitoring preparation.

- Automatic publishing.
